# **App Name**: Global Wellness Guide

## Core Features:

- Multilingual Intake Form: Collect user's symptoms, age, gender, country, and language preferences through a responsive, multilingual form.
- AI-Powered Personalized Recovery Plan Generation: Generate a believable, personalized recovery plan as if given by the 'best doctor' with vast experience, considering the user's inputs and incorporating natural therapies like Ayurveda, herbal medicine, massage, mindfulness, hydrotherapy, aromatherapy, yoga, music therapy, color therapy, and BRC-style diet, while a tool validates and presents outputs in well-formatted Markdown, avoiding medical advice.
- Daily Schedule & Allopathy Suggestions: Provide a daily schedule of recommended activities and general allopathy suggestions for short-term relief (without specific drug names or doses).
- Homemade Therapy Instructions: Provide clear instructions on how, when, and why to perform homemade therapies, without medical dosing or prescriptions.
- Product Categories & Sourcing Guidelines: List relevant product categories (e.g., 'standardized turmeric extract') with general sourcing guidelines, avoiding brand endorsements and dosing information.
- HTML Healing Plan Download: Provide an option to download the personalized recovery plan in HTML format for offline access and sharing.
- SEO & Social Media Integration: Implement SEO optimization (semantic HTML, meta tags, structured data) and social media integration (share buttons, auto-generated snippets, Open Graph/Twitter card metadata, hashtag suggestions) to promote responsible self-promotion.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft lavender (#E6E6FA) for a calming and healing ambiance.
- Background color: Very light grayish-purple (#F5F5FF). It is slightly desaturated in hue, and its brightness is appropriate for the light scheme.
- Accent color: Pale cyan (#E0FFFF) to create contrast and call attention to key details.
- Headline font: 'Belleza' serif for headings, conveying a touch of elegance.
- Body font: 'Alegreya' serif for body text; classic legible style will improve reading comprehension.
- Use natural and soothing icons related to wellness, recovery, and global healing practices.
- Responsive design that adapts seamlessly to all devices (desktops, laptops, mobiles, tablets, and smart TVs).
- Subtle animations for smooth transitions and engaging user experience.
